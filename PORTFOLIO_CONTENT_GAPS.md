# Portfolio evidence and visual checklist

Use this document to supply missing facts without overstating outcomes. Replace each bracketed prompt with confirmed information. If a fact cannot be confirmed, leave it out or label it as future direction.

## Content Operations Agent

### Confirmed information

- The UX design manager leading the myPepsiCo program first described the problem.
- Research and testing involved nine employees who create or support content alongside their primary jobs.
- Research to date includes three current-state interviews and four prototype demonstration and feedback sessions.
- Users were already using ChatGPT and Copilot to write articles. This directly led to adding a familiar conversational article-writing experience.
- PepsiCo article standards and acceptance criteria mattered, but were difficult for authors to follow and check manually.
- Users explicitly requested document upload and a more passive workflow in which the agents draft the article from source material.
- The product would be used infrequently, making familiar interaction patterns and low relearning effort essential.
- Content owners requested a broader admin dashboard with metrics and visibility into overall content health.
- Each user conversation either validated continued feature development or prompted a meaningful pivot; the prototype grew through continuous user-informed iteration.
- Shareable feedback includes: “This is super amazing—and super, super friendly. A great user experience.”
- Shareable feedback includes: “We can see our feedback influencing what was implemented since the last time we talked. This is so awesome.”
- Strongest demand signal: “How can we get this next week? This is going to provide so much value, and we need to get it out ASAP.”
- Canada has been proposed as the first real pilot, but the prototype remains too conceptual today. The team is developing it toward pilot readiness.
- Proposed pilot success criteria: faster article creation, less hands-on content-team support, and employees independently producing standards-aligned articles.
- Funding proposals are actively being written. There is no funding decision as of today.
- Validated now: AI-assisted article creation that helps meet PepsiCo standards.
- Future direction: end-to-end governance, administration, messaging, email notifications, and full PepsiCo workspace integration.

### Remaining information to supply

- Add the exact feature or workflow that was removed or completely redesigned after feedback, if there is a strong example beyond the additions documented above.
- Confirm whether the proposed Canada pilot gains an owner, target date, participant group, or approved plan.
- Replace the proposed success criteria with measurable thresholds once the pilot team defines them.
- Update funding status when the proposal receives a decision.

## Evidence standard for every case study

Each case study should answer these questions within the first screen and prove them below:

1. What complex problem did Grace solve?
2. What did she personally own?
3. Which design position or consequential decision did she establish?
4. What user or organizational evidence informed the work?
5. What changed because of the work?
6. Is the result shipped, validated, funded, or future-facing?

Use explicit status labels. Never describe potential reach as achieved impact.
