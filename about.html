I design the layer between people, data and decision-making.

I’m a senior UX designer at PepsiCo in Chicago, working on AI-native enterprise tools. My work is strongest where workflows are complex, stakes are high, and the product has to make information clear, usable and trustworthy. I studied UX design at Purdue University.

How I think

Start from the bottleneck

I treat user friction as a business diagnostic: work back from the operational bottleneck, find where people lose agency, and define what the product should change.

Strategy is knowing what not to build

I question the assumptions behind a feature list and sequence the work, so the first version earns the trust the next one needs.

Prototype the behavior

When guidelines don’t exist yet, I build working prototypes in React and TypeScript to test logic, flow and trust with real users and engineers.

Design trust into the product

In AI tools, provenance, confidence and human authority are product behavior, not explanatory copy.

Craft is removing noise

High craft in enterprise software means stripping away what competes with the decision, so attention goes where it matters.

How I work with AI

I design with AI, not only for it.

My workflow moves from Figma Make for quick exploration, to Figma for systems and detail, to Cursor, Codex and Claude Code with Figma MCP for working builds that reviewers and engineers can inspect.

I defined a point of view on UX within an AI-enabled development process and was invited by the VP of UX Design to develop phased implementation recommendations. I coach designers on AI-assisted prototyping, and I led a leadership-sponsored workshop for about 100 engineers on Figma MCP and AI coding tools.
