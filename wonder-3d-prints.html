My Role

I led the end-to-end design process, from understanding the brand and user needs to creating a custom ecommerce experience. I balanced creative expression with usability, ensuring the design felt authentic to Wonder 3D Prints while meeting ecommerce best practices for conversion and trust.

About the Project

Wonder 3D Prints creates unique, custom 3D printed products. Their brand is fun, creative, and approachable—but they needed an ecommerce site that could showcase this personality while building trust with customers making online purchases.

The design challenge: How do you make a creative brand feel authentic online while ensuring customers feel confident purchasing? Standard Shopify themes felt too generic and didn't capture the brand's unique character, but we also needed the reliability and functionality of a proven ecommerce platform.

The Solution

A fully custom ecommerce experience built on Shopify's reliable infrastructure, giving us complete design control to create a storefront that authentically represents the brand while maintaining the trust signals and functionality customers expect.

Key design decisions focused on balancing creativity with conversion: playful but professional, unique but familiar, creative but trustworthy.

Final Design

Lovable & Shopify Integration

The checkout process demonstrates the seamless connection between Lovable's custom frontend and Shopify's ecommerce backend. The custom design flows directly into Shopify's secure checkout system, showing how the two platforms work together.

Key Design Decisions

Brand Expression Through Custom Design

Standard Shopify themes couldn't capture Wonder 3D Prints' unique personality. I designed a completely custom storefront that:

  • Reflects the brand's fun, creative identity through custom layouts
  • Uses brand-specific design elements that feel authentic
  • Maintains ecommerce best practices for trust and conversion

User Experience Optimization

Focused on creating an intuitive shopping experience that guides users from discovery to purchase:

  • Clear product browsing with visual hierarchy
  • Streamlined checkout flow to reduce friction
  • Mobile-first responsive design for all devices
  • Trust signals integrated naturally into the design

Technical Implementation

Built a custom frontend connected to Shopify's proven ecommerce backend, giving us:

  • Complete design freedom without compromising functionality
  • Secure payment processing and order management
  • Reliable inventory tracking and fulfillment
  • Scalable architecture for future growth
